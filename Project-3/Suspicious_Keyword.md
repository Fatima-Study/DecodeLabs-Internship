# Suspicious Keyword Analysis

The project analyzes common keywords frequently used in phishing emails to identify potential security threats. These words are often used to create urgency, fear, or curiosity, encouraging recipients to click malicious links or disclose sensitive information. While these keywords may appear in legitimate emails, they should always be evaluated together with other phishing indicators before determining the overall risk.

| No. | Keyword | Potential Warning Sign |
|----:|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Urgent** | Creates pressure to act quickly. |
| 2 | **Immediately** | Discourages careful verification. |
| 3 | **Suspended** | Creates fear of losing access. |
| 4 | **Verify** | May lead to a fraudulent verification page. |
| 5 | **Password** | Indicates a potentially sensitive authentication target. |
| 6 | **Congratulations** | May be used to introduce a fake reward. |
| 7 | **Claim** | Encourages the recipient to interact with a link or request. |
| 8 | **Required** | Creates pressure to complete an action. |
| 9 | **Expire** | Creates a time-based sense of urgency. |
| 10 | **Click** | Directly encourages interaction with a destination. |
